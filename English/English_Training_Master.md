## Part 1: Self Introduction - 5 minutes

Possible questions:

* Tell me about yourself.
* Walk me through your experience.
* Why are you looking for a new opportunity?

* What was your role?
* What technologies did you use?
* What was the biggest challenge?

* What did you learn?

* What are your strengths?
* What are your weaknesses?
* Why should we hire you?
* What would you like to improve?

# Common Mistakes

## MuleSoft

Wrong:

* new software
* NewSoft
* NeoSoft
* Mewsoft

Correct:

* MuleSoft

---

## Past Tense

Wrong:

* I use
* We use
* We check
* We work

Correct:

* I used
* We used
* We checked
* We worked

---

## Old System

Wrong:

* older system

Correct:

* old system

---

## Modernize

Wrong:

* modernization system
* modernization process
* modernization of the system
* modern navigation system

Correct:

* to modernize the system

Example:

The goal was to modernize the system.

---

## Data Format

Wrong:

* data formatter
* data from right
* data from the end

Correct:

* data format

---

## Compatibility

Wrong:

* the compatibility

Correct:

* compatibility

# English Daily - Day13

## Goal

30-minute technical interview training (AI track).

Focus:
- Explain an AI project using simple English.
- Explain problems, challenges, and solutions.
- Use short sentences.

---

## AI Project

### Q1 Tell me about your AI project?

Correct Answer:

This was a RAG demo system.

Users can upload documents and ask questions.

---

### Q2 What can the system do?

Correct Answer:

This system can read PDF documents and answer questions.

---

### Q3 What technologies did you use?

Correct Answer:

I used Node.js, TypeScript, and Python.

I also used vector search and embeddings.

---

### Q4 What problem does your AI project solve?

Correct Answer:

It helps users find information from documents.

Users do not need to read the whole document.

---

### Q5 What was your role?

Correct Answer:

My role was system design, backend development, and testing.

---

### Q6 What was the biggest challenge?

Simple Version:

The biggest challenge was finding the right information.

Sometimes the system could not find the right information.

Interview Version:

The biggest challenge was retrieval accuracy.

Sometimes the system could not find the right information.

---

### Q7 How did you solve it?

Correct Answer:

I improved the document processing.

I changed the search settings.

After that, the results became better.

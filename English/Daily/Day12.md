# 30-Minute Interview Structure

## Part 1: Self Introduction - 5 minutes

Possible questions:

- Tell me about yourself.
- Walk me through your experience.
- Why are you looking for a new opportunity?

## Part 2: Project Experience - 10 minutes

Possible questions:

- Tell me about your recent project.
- What was your role?
- What technologies did you use?
- What was the biggest challenge?
- How did you solve it?
- What did you learn?

## Part 3: Teamwork - 5 minutes

Possible questions:

- How did you work with other teams?
- How do you communicate with customers?
- How do you handle pressure?

## Part 4: AI Experience - 5-10 minutes

Possible questions:

- Tell me about your AI project.
- What problem does it solve?
- What technologies did you use?
- What challenges did you face?
- What did you learn?

## Part 5: Strength and Future Plan - 5 minutes

Possible questions:

- What are your strengths?
- What are your weaknesses?
- Why should we hire you?
- What would you like to improve?

# Day12 Practice Summary

## Q1 Tell me about yourself

### User Answer

My name is Zhou Yongxin.

I have over 20 years of experience in the IT industry, including more than 10 years of experience in full-stack system developer and over 8 years in system management and operation.

I work mainly with the system business system.

I use Java, Spring Boot, Newsoft, and enjoy solving problems and work with different teams.

### Corrected Version

My name is Zhou Yongxin, and you can call me John.

I have over 20 years of experience in the IT industry.

I have more than 10 years of experience as a full-stack developer, and over 8 years in system maintenance and operation.

I mainly worked on business systems.

I use Java, Spring Boot, and MuleSoft.

I enjoy solving problems and working with different teams.

### Notes

Need improvement:
- full-stack system developer -> a full-stack developer
- system management and operation -> system maintenance and operation
- system business system -> business systems
- Newsoft -> MuleSoft

## Q2 Tell me about your recent project

### New Word

recent = 最近的 / 近期的

Similar expressions:

- recent project
- latest project
- last project

### User Answer

It was a modernization project.

The old system was built with PHP, and used the new software integrating APIs.

### Corrected Version

It was a modernization project.

The old system was built with PHP.

We used MuleSoft to integrate APIs.

### Notes

Need improvement:
- new software -> MuleSoft
- integrating APIs -> to integrate APIs

# Important Observation

The biggest current weakness is not only grammar.

The main weakness is:

**Not enough verbs.**

The user often uses:

- use
- work
- check
- test

This is useful, but not enough for a 30-minute technical interview.

---

# Verb Training Plan

From Day13, add 5 high-frequency technical interview verbs every day.

## Day13 Verbs

- build = 构建
- use = 使用
- retrieve = 检索
- generate = 生成
- improve = 改进

Example sentences:

I built a RAG demo system.

I used Node.js and Python.

The system retrieves relevant information.

The system generates answers.

I improved retrieval accuracy.
